隐私政策

失物认领处 · 7 号甲板 / Lost & Found: Deck Seven　·　版本 1.2　·　最后更新：2026-11-14

本游戏由个人开发者 Liu Zonghao / GR studio（下称"我们"）发布。我们只收集法规要求必须收集的信息， 其余一切留在你的设备上。

1. 我们收集什么

游戏本身不收集任何个人信息，也没有账号体系。以下数据只保存在你的设备本地：

  • 游戏进度（天数、信任、站币、已判案件、物品图鉴）—— 保存在 localStorage
  • 设置项（音量、扫描线、语言、减少动效）—— 同样只保存在本地

卸载应用或在系统设置里清除数据，这些内容即全部删除，我们无法访问。

2. 实名认证与防沉迷

根据国家新闻出版署《关于进一步严格管理切实防止未成年人沉迷网络游戏的通知》， 进入游戏前需要完成实名认证。本作接入 TapTap 合规认证服务（TapSDK） 完成这一流程：

  • 提交的信息：姓名与身份证号。由 TapSDK 直接提交至 TapTap 合规认证服务， 并按其规则上报国家网络游戏防沉迷实名认证系统。
  • 我们不做的事：游戏本身不接收、不存储、不上传你的姓名与身份证号， 也不建立任何玩家数据库。
  • 未成年人保护：实名结果为未成年人时，游戏时段与时长按上述通知执行 （仅周五、周六、周日及法定节假日 20:00–21:00 可玩）；被限制时游戏会直接拦下并说明原因。
  • 拒绝实名的结果：无法进入游戏。
  • 第三方说明：TapTap 合规认证服务由 TapTap（易玩网络）提供， 其个人信息处理规则见 TapTap 隐私政策。

3. 网络、广告与统计

  • 本游戏不包含广告，未接入任何广告平台、分析 SDK、社交分享或推送服务
  • 游戏运行时的网络请求只用于上面第 2 节的实名认证与防沉迷，不上传任何游戏进度或设备信息
  • Android 安装包声明两项权限：INTERNET（合规认证联网）、 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE（判断网络是否可用）
  • 未申请位置、通讯录、相册、摄像头、麦克风、短信等任何敏感权限

4. AI 使用声明

本作的程序代码、部分叙事文案与像素美术在开发阶段使用了 AI 辅助生成，随后由开发者逐项校对与调整。 游戏运行时不调用任何生成式 AI 服务，包内美术、文本、音频均为预置资源，随包发布。

5. 儿童

本游戏面向 12 岁及以上用户。除第 2 节所述的实名认证信息外，我们不收集任何个人信息； 该实名信息同样由 TapSDK 处理，不进入我们的系统。

6. 变更

若本政策有更新，我们会修改本页顶部的版本号与日期。继续使用即视为接受更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy (English)

Lost & Found: Deck Seven　·　v1.2　·　Last updated: 14 Nov 2026

Published by Liu Zonghao / GR studio. We collect only what the law requires us to collect; everything else stays on your device.

What we collect

The game itself collects no personal data and has no account system. Progress (day, trust, credits, resolved cases, item codex) and settings (volume, scanlines, language, reduced motion) are stored only in localStorage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them; we cannot access them.

Real-name verification & anti-addiction

Chinese regulation requires real-name verification before play. This game uses the TapTap Compliance service (TapSDK) for that flow:

  • Data submitted: name and national ID number, submitted by TapSDK directly to the TapTap Compliance service and reported to the national anti-addiction system as required.
  • What we do not do: the game never receives, stores or uploads your name or ID number, and keeps no player database.
  • Minors: play time is limited to Friday, Saturday, Sunday and public holidays, 20:00–21:00; the game blocks play outside that window and explains why.
  • If you decline: the game cannot be entered.
  • Third party: see TapTap's privacy policy.

Network, ads and analytics

The game contains no advertising and integrates no ad network, analytics SDK, social sharing or push. Network requests are made only for the real-name / anti-addiction flow described above, and no gameplay or device data is uploaded. The Android build declares INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE only; no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or SMS permissions are requested.

AI disclosure

Code, part of the narrative text and the pixel art were produced with AI assistance during development, then reviewed by the developer. No generative AI runs inside the game; all art, text and audio are shipped with the package.

Children

Intended for users aged 12 and over. Apart from the verification data described above — which is processed by TapSDK and never enters our systems — no personal data is collected.
